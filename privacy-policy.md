# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the LinkedIn-connected automation application associated with this project handles information when a user authorizes the application.

## Information the application may process

The application may process only the information needed to provide the features the user explicitly authorizes, which may include:

- basic account or profile identifiers made available through the platform authorization flow;
- authorization tokens or permissions required to connect the user's account;
- content the user chooses to review, approve, or publish through the automation workflow;
- technical records needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the workflow.

## How information is used

Information is used only to operate the authorized automation, including preparing content for review, storing workflow records, and publishing content when the user has explicitly approved that action.

The application does not sell personal information and does not use connected-account data for unrelated advertising or profiling.

## Authentication and credentials

Platform access is handled through the platform's authorization mechanisms and the connected automation environment. Users should not be asked to share their account password with this application.

## Data sharing

Information may be processed by the services required to run the workflow, such as the connected automation platform, database/workspace services, AI services, and the social platform being used. Data is shared only as necessary to provide the requested workflow functionality.

## Data retention

Workflow records may be retained for operational, review, audit, and troubleshooting purposes. Users may disconnect the application or revoke platform access at any time through the relevant platform settings.

## User choices

Users can stop using the application, revoke its authorization, or request deletion of data associated with their use of the workflow.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and workflow data. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or connected services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
