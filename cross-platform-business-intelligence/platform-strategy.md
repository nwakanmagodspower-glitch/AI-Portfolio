# Platform Strategy

## Principle

The system shares intelligence across platforms, not identical copy.

## Reddit

Role:
- discovery of concrete founder/operator problems
- community replies and educational posts where rules permit

Content style:
- conversational
- specific to the actual problem
- explains diagnosis and practical process change
- avoids hard selling
- community rules checked before posting

Strong formats:
- problem breakdown
- helpful reply
- process explanation
- before/after operating model

## X

Role:
- current conversation discovery
- fast distribution of sharp business insights

Content style:
- concise
- strong first sentence
- one main idea per post
- short thread only when necessary
- diagrams can become simple image cards later

Strong formats:
- one-paragraph insight
- short list of process steps
- mini-thread
- observation + lesson

## LinkedIn

Role:
- professional authority and distribution
- later, authorized owned comments/replies/performance can feed new intelligence

Content style:
- professional business framing
- structured paragraphs
- explains operational implications
- stronger emphasis on management, teams, systems, customer experience, and business outcomes

Strong formats:
- thought-leadership post
- operating lesson
- before/after process
- diagram/document post

## Platform Fit Logic

A Master Insight receives three scores from 0-100.

Suggested interpretation:
- 80-100: strong native fit
- 60-79: usable with careful adaptation
- below 60: normally skip

No platform is mandatory. The system should not force a topic onto all three.

## Cost Strategy

1. Research and diagnose once.
2. Store Master Insight.
3. Generate all strong-fit platform variants in one structured AI request when possible.
4. Reuse the stored Master Insight for future channels or rewrites.

## Example

Master lesson:
`More leads do not solve a broken follow-up process.`

Reddit version:
A deeper explanation of why leads disappear and how ownership/next-action systems help.

X version:
A short observation such as: `More leads won't fix broken follow-up. If the next action lives in someone's memory, interested prospects will disappear.`

LinkedIn version:
A professional operations post explaining why sales teams need visible ownership, next actions, reminders, and outcomes.

The lesson is the same. The social behavior is different.
