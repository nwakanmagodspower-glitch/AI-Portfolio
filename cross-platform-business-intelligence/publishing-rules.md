# Cross-Platform Publishing Rules

## Universal rules

- Teach first.
- Diagnose before prescribing.
- Benefits before technology.
- No invented experience, results, statistics, case studies, revenue, savings, ROI, or guarantees.
- No hard sell by default.
- No automated unsolicited DMs.
- Do not mass-post substantially identical content.
- Human approval is required before publishing in Version 1.
- Platform-specific rules and permissions override generic system behavior.

## Reddit

- Check community rules before a post or reply.
- Do not repeat promotional links across communities.
- Do not automate votes or karma manipulation.
- Prefer helpful participation over self-promotion.

## X

- Avoid repetitive near-duplicate posting.
- Keep the main insight concise and useful.
- Use threads only when the idea genuinely needs expansion.
- Do not manufacture engagement or coordinated amplification.

## LinkedIn

- Use authorized API routes only.
- Do not treat unofficial scraping as the production foundation.
- Prefer professional, business-outcome framing.
- Owned comments/replies may feed future content intelligence where authorized access permits.

## Cross-platform rule

The same Master Insight may be approved for one, two, or all three platforms. Approval is per platform version.

`Approve All` is allowed only when every individual platform version has passed its own rule/quality check.

## Diagram rule

A diagram is shared conceptually but may be reformatted for each platform. It should normally contain 4-8 business-language steps and one clear lesson.
